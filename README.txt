SOLO LEVELING SYSTEM
====================
Files (all in one folder): index.html (app), menu.mp3, check.wav, logo.jpg, icon-180/192/512.png, manifest.json, sw.js

Install on your phone (best option, works offline afterwards)
1. Upload all these files together to any free host: Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop), GitHub Pages or Vercel.
2. Open the link on your phone.
3. Android Chrome: menu > Add to Home screen. iPhone Safari: Share > Add to Home Screen.

Make an APK: put your hosted link into pwabuilder.com and download the Android package.

Quick test: double-click index.html on a computer. It works, but it cannot be installed from a local file.

Notes
- Progress is saved in the browser on that device.
- After you change any file, change C='sl-v1' to 'sl-v2' in sw.js so installed copies update.
